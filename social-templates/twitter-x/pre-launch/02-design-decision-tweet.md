# Twitter/X — Design Decision Single Tweet / Mini-Thread

**Use when:** You shipped a feature that reflects anti-fetishization thinking. Single tweet for simple decisions; 3–5 tweet thread for ones that need explanation.
**Cadence:** ~1 per week through pre-launch.

---

## Single tweet (most common)

```
Design decision in [APP NAME]:

[ONE-LINE STATEMENT OF THE DECISION — e.g., "You can't send a generic like. Every like requires a comment on a specific photo or prompt."]

Why: [ONE-LINE WHY — e.g., "An inbox of low-effort taps is the #1 thing trans women told me makes apps unusable."]

[OPTIONAL: SCREEN RECORDING OR SCREENSHOT]
```

---

## Mini-thread (for decisions that need 2–3 tweets of context)

> **1/**
> Design decision in Amia: [ONE-LINE STATEMENT].
>
> Here's why. 🧵

> **2/**
> The status quo: [ONE-PARAGRAPH DESCRIPTION OF HOW OTHER APPS HANDLE THIS, AND WHAT BREAKS BECAUSE OF IT].

> **3/**
> What we shipped instead: [SPECIFIC IMPLEMENTATION].
>
> [ADVISOR FIRST NAME] flagged this in week [N]. We changed it because of [SPECIFIC FEEDBACK].

> **4/**
> If this resonates and you want to see more — waitlist: [LINK].

---

## Notes

- Single tweets perform better than threads for this content type — only thread if there's actually 200+ words of useful detail.
- Always include a screen recording or screenshot. The visual proof matters.
- Don't compare to specific competitor apps by name. Implied is fine; named is punching.
