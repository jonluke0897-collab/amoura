# TikTok — Sunday Week-In-Review (Day 7)

**Use when:** End of launch week. Honest recap. Specific numbers where you can share, specific lessons where you can't.
**Length:** 60–90 seconds, face-to-camera.

---

## Script

> **Hook:**
> "Week one of Amia — what actually happened."

> **Beat 2 — The numbers (only what you can share):**
> "[N] people downloaded. [N]% completed their profile. [N] matches happened. [N] of you sent me feedback I needed to hear."

> **Beat 3 — What worked:**
> "The thing I was most nervous about — [SPECIFIC THING, e.g., 'the like-with-comment gate slowing things down too much'] — actually worked. Users told me [SPECIFIC FEEDBACK]."

> **Beat 4 — What didn't:**
> "The thing I was most confident about — [SPECIFIC THING] — needs more work. [SPECIFIC ISSUE]. I'm fixing it next week."

> **Beat 5 — What's shipping next week:**
> "Three things shipping in the next 7 days: [THING 1], [THING 2], [THING 3]."

> **Beat 6 — Close:**
> "If you tried Amia this week — thank you. If you didn't — link's in bio. We're in [CITIES] and growing."

---

## Caption

```
Week 1 recap.

What worked: [ONE LINE].
What didn't: [ONE LINE].
What's shipping next week: [ONE LINE].

Honest weekly recaps every Sunday.

#amia #buildinpublic #trans #t4t
```

## Notes

- If a number isn't flattering, share the lesson, not the number. Not lying — just protecting metrics that aren't ready for public yet.
- Run the script past at least one advisor before filming.
- This format becomes a recurring weekly post in post-launch growth — see `post-launch-growth/03-weekly-shipped-recap.md`.
