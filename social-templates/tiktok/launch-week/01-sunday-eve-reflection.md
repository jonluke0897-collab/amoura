# TikTok — Sunday Eve Reflection (T-1 day to launch)

**Use when:** The night before public launch. NOT a hype post. A reflection.
**Post time:** 8pm PT.
**Length:** 60 seconds, face-to-camera, no music.

---

## Script

> **Hook:**
> "Tomorrow Amia goes public. Tonight I want to thank the people who got it here."

> **Beat 2 — Name advisors (with permission):**
> "[ADVISOR 1 FULL NAME], [ADVISOR 2 FULL NAME], [ADVISOR 3 FULL NAME] — the three trans advisors who reviewed every piece of copy, every flow, every moderation rule in this app. They are the reason this works."

> **Beat 3 — Name the first ten beta testers (anonymized or by first name with permission):**
> "And to the [NUMBER] beta users who used Amia for the last [N] weeks — [LIST OF FIRST NAMES OR 'you know who you are'] — you broke the things I needed broken. Thank you."

> **Beat 4 — The honest goal:**
> "I'm not trying to be the biggest dating app. I'm trying to build something trans women actually recommend to their friends. That's the whole bar."

> **Beat 5 — Close:**
> "Tomorrow at 7am Pacific. Link in bio."
