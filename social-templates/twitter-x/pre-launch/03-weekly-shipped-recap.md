# Twitter/X — Weekly Shipped Recap (Pre-launch)

**Use when:** End of each pre-launch week. Single tweet OR short thread.
**Cadence:** Every Friday or Sunday, weeks -8 through -1.

---

## Single tweet version

```
Week [N] of building [APP NAME] — what shipped:

✅ [SHIPPED THING 1]
✅ [SHIPPED THING 2]
✅ [SHIPPED THING 3]

🔧 [ONE THING I'M CHANGING NEXT WEEK BASED ON ADVISOR / USER FEEDBACK]

Roadmap: [LINK TO product-roadmap.md]
```

---

## Thread version (for weeks with bigger lessons)

> **1/**
> Week [N] of building [APP NAME]. Quick recap. 🧵

> **2/**
> Shipped this week:
> – [THING 1] ([WHY IT MATTERS IN 6 WORDS])
> – [THING 2] ([WHY IT MATTERS])
> – [THING 3] ([WHY IT MATTERS])

> **3/**
> One thing that didn't go to plan: [SPECIFIC THING]. [WHAT YOU LEARNED]. [WHAT YOU'RE CHANGING].

> **4/**
> One thing an advisor flagged: [PARAPHRASED FEEDBACK FROM ADVISOR — credit by first name with permission]. We're [SPECIFIC RESPONSE] next week.

> **5/**
> Waitlist now at [N]. Soft launch in [N] weeks.
>
> Sign up: [LINK]

---

## Notes

- The honesty is the engagement, not the shipped count. A week with 1 shipped item and 1 thoughtful lesson outperforms a week with 5 shipped items and no reflection.
- Don't fabricate "what didn't go to plan." Skip the section if nothing did.
