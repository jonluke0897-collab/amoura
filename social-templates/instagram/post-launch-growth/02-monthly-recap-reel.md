# Instagram — Monthly Recap Reel

**Use when:** Cross-post of the TikTok monthly metric recap, recut for IG.
**Length:** 60–75 seconds.

---

## Script

(Same beats as `tiktok/post-launch-growth/03-monthly-metric-recap.md`.)

> **Hook:**
> "Month [N] of [APP NAME] — what the numbers actually said."

> **Beat 2 — Growth metric:**
> "[METRIC] is at [VALUE], up [%] from last month."

> **Beat 3 — Activation metric:**
> "[METRIC] is [VALUE]. We're [ABOVE / BELOW] target because [REASON]."

> **Beat 4 — User feedback theme:**
> "Top theme from in-app feedback this month: [ONE-LINE THEME]."

> **Beat 5 — What's changing:**
> "Because of that, [SPECIFIC SHIPPING DECISION] is the priority for month [N+1]."

> **Beat 6 — Close:**
> "Full breakdown on [BLOG / TWITTER]. Honest monthly recaps for as long as I run this."

---

## Caption

```
Month [N] recap.

📈 [GROWTH METRIC]: [VALUE] ([CHANGE])
🎯 [ACTIVATION METRIC]: [VALUE]
🗣 [USER THEME]
🔧 Shipping next: [ONE-LINE]

Full breakdown: link in bio.

#amia #buildinpublic #trans
```

## Notes

- IG audience is more visual; consider a 3-frame stat overlay on the reel.
- Pair the reel with a Story carousel that surfaces individual stats one at a time.
