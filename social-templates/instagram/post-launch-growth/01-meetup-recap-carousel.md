# Instagram — Meetup Recap Carousel

**Use when:** After a launch-city meetup. The visual companion to the TikTok meetup recap.
**Format:** 6–8 slides, mix of B-roll photos and pull quotes.

---

## Slide structure

### Slide 1 — Cover
> **Headline:** "[APP NAME] x [CITY]"
> **Sub:** "[DATE] · [VENUE NAME, with consent] · [N] people"
> **Visual:** Wide-shot of the room (no faces, OR only with explicit consent).

### Slide 2 — The setup
> **Visual:** Hands, drinks, the bar, the venue's exterior.
> **Caption text:** "Hosted by [@LOCAL CO-HOST WITH PERMISSION] at [@VENUE WITH PERMISSION]."

### Slides 3–5 — Pull quotes
> **Format:** Large-type pull quotes from things people said in the room.
> **Body:** "[PARAPHRASED OR VERBATIM-WITH-CONSENT QUOTE]"
> *(One per slide. Anonymized unless explicit consent.)*

### Slide 6 — What we're changing
> **Headline:** "Because of last night:"
> **Body:** "[SPECIFIC THING SHIPPING THIS WEEK]"

### Slide 7 — Next meetup
> **Headline:** "Next up: [CITY]"
> **Sub:** "[DATE] · [VENUE if confirmed] · RSVP in bio"

### Slide 8 — Credit
> **Body:** "Thank you to [@CO-HOST], [@VENUE], and everyone who came."

---

## Caption

```
[CITY] meetup last night. [N] people. [VENUE @HANDLE] — thanks for hosting.

What I heard → [ONE-LINE THEME].
What I'm shipping → [ONE-LINE].

Next: [CITY], [DATE]. RSVP in bio.

#amia #[cityhashtag] #trans #t4t #queerlove
```

---

## Notes

- No faces without signed consent. B-roll of hands, drinks, and the room is plenty.
- Tag the venue and co-host. Pay the co-host.
- Counts toward the ≥ 2:1 amplification ratio.
