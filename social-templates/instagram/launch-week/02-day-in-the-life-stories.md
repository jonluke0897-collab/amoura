# Instagram — Launch Week "Day in the Life" Stories

**Use when:** Daily Stories during launch week. Lower production, higher frequency than reels.
**Cadence:** 4–7 frames per day, Mon–Fri of launch week.

---

## Daily template

### Morning frame (8am PT)
> **Visual:** Phone or laptop with the app open OR coffee + notebook.
> **Sticker text:** "Day [N] of launch. [ONE-LINE GOAL FOR TODAY — e.g., 'shipping the photo upload fix']"

### Midday frame (12pm PT)
> **Visual:** Screenshot of one specific support DM (with sender's name covered).
> **Sticker text:** "Got this from a user this morning. This is why we're doing this."
> *(Only post if you have explicit consent. Otherwise paraphrase.)*

### Afternoon frame (3pm PT)
> **Visual:** Screen recording of a feature you're working on.
> **Sticker text:** "Shipping this afternoon: [WHAT]"

### Evening frame (8pm PT)
> **Visual:** End-of-day reflection, face-to-camera, 15 seconds.
> **Sticker text:** "Day [N] done. Hardest part: [ONE LINE]. Best part: [ONE LINE]."

### Pinned link sticker (every frame)
> "Download Amia → [LINK]"

---

## Notes

- Don't post Stories on Saturday — protect the rest day.
- Save the best Stories to a "Launch Week" highlight on the profile.
- Never post DMs/screenshots from users without their explicit consent. When in doubt, paraphrase instead.
