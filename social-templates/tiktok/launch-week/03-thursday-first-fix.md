# TikTok — Thursday First Fix (Day 4)

**Use when:** You shipped the first iteration after 3 days of real-user data. Post in the afternoon, after the fix is live.
**Length:** 30–45 seconds, face-to-camera, can include a quick screen-share.

---

## Script

> **Hook:**
> "Day 4 of launch. Here's the first thing I fixed because of what I saw this week."

> **Beat 2 — The friction you saw:**
> "[N]% of people were dropping off at [SPECIFIC STEP — e.g., 'the photo upload step in onboarding']. [ONE-SENTENCE WHY — e.g., 'the camera permission prompt was hitting before users understood what photos were for']."

> **Beat 3 — The fix:**
> "I [SPECIFIC ACTION — e.g., 'moved the explanation above the permission prompt and added a one-line preview of how photos get used']. Drop-off went from [N]% to [M]% in the first hour after I shipped."

> **Beat 4 — The promise:**
> "This is the whole loop — watch what's breaking, fix it the same week. I'll keep posting fixes."

## Notes

- Only post if the fix actually shipped and you have real before/after numbers.
