# TikTok — Monthly Metric Recap

**Use when:** End of every month, post-launch. The "abbreviated NPS + lessons" version of GTM § 5's monthly publish.
**Length:** 60–90 seconds, face-to-camera.

---

## Script

> **Hook:**
> "Month [N] of Amia — here's what the numbers actually said."

> **Beat 2 — One growth metric:**
> "[METRIC NAME — e.g., 'Weekly active trans / non-binary users'] is at [NUMBER], up [%] from last month."

> **Beat 3 — One activation metric:**
> "[METRIC NAME — e.g., 'Completed-profile rate'] is [%]. Target is [%]. We're [ABOVE / BELOW] target because [SPECIFIC REASON]."

> **Beat 4 — One thing users told us:**
> "Top theme from in-app NPS this month: [ONE-LINE PARAPHRASED THEME]. Mentioned by [N] of [TOTAL] respondents."

> **Beat 5 — One thing we're changing:**
> "Because of that, [SPECIFIC SHIPPING DECISION] is the priority for month [N+1]."

> **Beat 6 — Close:**
> "Full recap on [BLOG / TWITTER]. Honest monthly recaps for as long as I run this."

---

## Caption

```
Month [N] recap:

📈 [GROWTH METRIC]: [VALUE] ([CHANGE])
🎯 [ACTIVATION METRIC]: [VALUE] (target [TARGET])
🗣 Top user theme: [ONE-LINE]
🔧 Shipping next: [ONE-LINE]

Full breakdown on [LINK].

#amia #buildinpublic #trans
```

## Notes

- Share metrics you've committed to share. Don't open new ones reactively.
- If a number is bad, share the diagnosis and the action — not just the number.
- Don't share metrics that could expose individual users (e.g., never any per-city subscriber counts < 50).
