# Twitter/X — Weekly Shipped Recap (Post-Launch)

**Use when:** Recurring weekly Sunday recap. Same shape as the launch-week recap, condensed.
**Cadence:** Every Sunday.
**Length:** Single tweet most weeks; thread on weeks with bigger lessons.

---

## Single tweet (default)

```
Week [N] of [APP NAME] in public:

✅ Shipped: [THING 1], [THING 2], [THING 3]
📈 [METRIC]: [VALUE] ([CHANGE])
🗣 Top user feedback: [ONE-LINE PARAPHRASED THEME]
🔧 Fixing next: [ONE-LINE]

[LINK]
```

---

## Thread version (for bigger weeks)

> **1/**
> Week [N] of [APP NAME]. Things that mattered. 🧵

> **2/ Shipped.**
> – [THING 1] ([WHY])
> – [THING 2] ([WHY])
> – [THING 3] ([WHY])

> **3/ Numbers.**
> [METRIC 1]: [VALUE] ([CHANGE])
> [METRIC 2]: [VALUE] ([CHANGE])
> [METRIC 3]: [VALUE] ([CHANGE])

> **4/ Lesson.**
> [SPECIFIC THING I LEARNED THIS WEEK FROM USERS / ADVISORS / DATA]. [WHAT IT'S CHANGING NEXT WEEK].

> **5/ Next.**
> Shipping next week: [THING 1], [THING 2].
>
> [LINK]

---

## Notes

- The repetition of cadence is the brand. Skipping a week reads worse than skipping content.
- Don't gamify the streak — if a week was quiet, say so.
