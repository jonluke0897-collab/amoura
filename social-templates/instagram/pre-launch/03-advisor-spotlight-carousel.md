# Instagram — Advisor Spotlight Carousel

**Use when:** Spotlighting a paid advisor (with their written consent). Mirror of the Twitter/X advisor announcement, visual format.
**Format:** 4–6 slides.

---

## Slide structure

### Slide 1 — Cover
> **Headline:** "Meet [ADVISOR FIRST NAME]"
> **Sub:** "Paid advisor on [APP NAME]"
> **Visual:** Photo only with their explicit written consent. Otherwise: typography-only with their name.

### Slide 2 — Who they are
> **Body:** "[ADVISOR'S OWN BIO — IN THEIR WORDS, NOT YOURS]"
> **Visual:** Quiet design, brand palette.

### Slide 3 — What they bring
> **Headline:** "What [FIRST NAME] reviews:"
> **Body:** "[SPECIFIC AREA — e.g., 'Moderation policy + report-resolution copy']"

### Slide 4 — A direct quote
> **Visual:** Pull quote, large type. Verbatim and approved by the advisor.
> **Body:** "[ADVISOR-APPROVED QUOTE]"

### Slide 5 — Pay receipt
> **Headline:** "We pay our advisors."
> **Body:** "[FIRST NAME] is paid [TRANSPARENT RATE] for the work. If you can't afford to pay trans expertise, you're not ready to build for trans users."

### Slide 6 — Amplify their work
> **Headline:** "Find [FIRST NAME]:"
> **Body:** "[THEIR HANDLE / NEWSLETTER / SHOP / WHATEVER THEY WANT AMPLIFIED]"

---

```
Spotlight on [@ADVISOR HANDLE WITH PERMISSION] — paid advisor on Amia.
```

## Notes

- Written, signed consent before this goes live. Default to no.
- Let the advisor approve the final draft of every slide before posting.
- Pay separately for the appearance (on top of retainer).
- Counts toward the ≥ 2:1 amplification ratio.
