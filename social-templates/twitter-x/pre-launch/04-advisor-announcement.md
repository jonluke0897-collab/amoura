# Twitter/X — Advisor Announcement / Spotlight

**Use when:** Publicly announcing a new advisor (with their permission), or amplifying advisor work. Counts toward the ≥ 2:1 amplification ratio.
**Cadence:** ~1 per month minimum.

---

## Announcement single tweet

```
[ADVISOR FULL NAME, @HANDLE WITH PERMISSION] is the [Nth] paid advisor on [APP NAME].

She/he/they will be reviewing [SPECIFIC AREA — e.g., "moderation policy and report-resolution copy"].

I'm paying [HER/HIM/THEM] [TRANSPARENT RATE].

Pay trans expertise. Always.
```

---

## Spotlight thread (when amplifying their work)

> **1/**
> Spotlight on [ADVISOR NAME] — [ADVISOR HANDLE WITH PERMISSION] — paid advisor on [APP NAME] since week [N]. 🧵

> **2/**
> [ADVISOR NAME] is [PROFESSION / PRACTICE / CONTEXT — IN THE WORDS THEY'D USE]. [ONE-LINE ABOUT WHAT THEY BRING].

> **3/**
> Specific contributions to the product:
> – [SPECIFIC THING THEY PUSHED FOR]
> – [SPECIFIC THING THEY PUSHED BACK ON]
> – [SPECIFIC PIECE OF COPY OR FEATURE THEY OWN]

> **4/**
> Direct quote (with permission): "[VERBATIM ADVISOR-APPROVED QUOTE]."

> **5/**
> [ADVISOR FIRST NAME]'s work outside [APP NAME]: [LINK TO THEIR BLOG / NEWSLETTER / SHOP / WHATEVER THEY WANT AMPLIFIED].

> **6/**
> We pay our advisors. If you can't afford to pay trans expertise, you're not ready to build for trans users.

---

## Notes

- Written, signed consent before naming. Default to no.
- Always include their handle if they want it tagged, and amplify something of theirs (not just the work-on-Amia piece).
- Pay separately for the appearance — on top of retainer.
