# TikTok — Meetup Recap

**Use when:** After hosting a launch-city meetup (per GTM § 5 and § 8). 2–3 TikToks per meetup.
**Length:** 30–60 seconds, mix of B-roll from the event + face-to-camera reflection.

---

## Script

> **Hook:**
> "We had [N] trans women in a room together last night in [CITY] talking about dating."

> **Beat 2 — Set the scene:**
> "Hosted by [LOCAL CO-HOST FIRST NAME, IF THEY CONSENT] at [QUEER-OWNED VENUE NAME, IF THEY CONSENT]. [N] people. Free drinks, [SPECIFIC FOOD], no pitch deck."

> **Beat 3 — One thing you heard (paraphrased, anonymized unless permission):**
> "One person said something I haven't been able to stop thinking about: [PARAPHRASED QUOTE — e.g., 'most apps make me feel like a category. This one made me feel like a person']."

> **Beat 4 — One thing you're changing because of it:**
> "Because of conversations like that one, [SPECIFIC THING THAT'S CHANGING NEXT WEEK]."

> **Beat 5 — Close:**
> "Next meetup: [CITY], [DATE]. Free, small, queer-owned venue. RSVP in bio."

---

## Caption

```
[CITY] meetup last night. [N] people. [QUEER-OWNED VENUE] — thanks for hosting.

What I heard: [ONE-LINE].
What I'm changing: [ONE-LINE].

Next: [CITY] on [DATE]. RSVP in bio.

#amia #[CITY] #trans #t4t
```

## Notes

- B-roll: hands, drinks, room shots. Never faces without explicit signed consent.
- Pay the co-host. Tag the venue.
- This counts toward the ≥ 2:1 amplification ratio (GTM § 8) since the venue and co-host are amplified.
