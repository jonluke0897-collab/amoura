# Amia — Social Media Post Templates

Fill-in-the-blank templates derived from `docs/gtm.md`. Use them as scaffolding, not scripts. Voice and stance should always come from you (and from your advisors).

## How to use

1. Pick the platform folder (`tiktok/`, `twitter-x/`, `instagram/`, `reddit/`).
2. Pick the phase (`pre-launch/`, `launch-week/`, `post-launch-growth/`).
3. Open the template, swap every `[BRACKETED PLACEHOLDER]` for your specifics, and delete the directorial notes (`> Notes:` blocks).
4. Run anything that touches trans experience past at least one paid advisor before posting (per `docs/gtm.md § 8` and the advisor gate in `CLAUDE.md`).

## Voice rules (lifted from `docs/gtm.md § 7`)

- Warm, specific, not preachy. Has an edge on transphobia.
- Every post ladders to one of three narratives:
  1. The trans dating landscape is broken in **specific** ways.
  2. Here is one **specific** thing I'm doing to fix it.
  3. Here is one **specific** thing I got wrong and changed.
- Specificity beats vibes. "We made sending a like require a comment on a specific photo or prompt" > "We made our app respectful."

## Off-limits content

- No screenshots of bad behavior on competitor apps (punching down).
- No statistics about violence against trans people as marketing (exploitative).
- No "look how inclusive we are" declarations — show it through product decisions.
- Never call yourself an "ally."
- Never use stock footage of trans people. Never use AI-generated imagery of trans people.
- Featured users are paid ($100–$200 per feature) and have written consent.

## Cadence reference (from GTM § 7)

| Channel | Cadence |
|---|---|
| TikTok | 2–3 videos/week, 30–90s, face-to-camera |
| Twitter/X | 3 posts/week (single tweets + 5–10-tweet threads) |
| Instagram | Light cross-posting only, not primary |
| Reddit | Participate first, post second. ~2–3 hours/week of genuine participation |

## Advisor review checklist

Before posting anything that touches trans experience:

- [ ] Read aloud — does the tone sound performative?
- [ ] At least one paid advisor reviewed the copy in writing.
- [ ] Specifics over generalities — what concrete decision/result/lesson?
- [ ] Not reactive to a competitor. Not defensive.
- [ ] Amplification ratio still ≥ 2:1 this week (per GTM § 8).
