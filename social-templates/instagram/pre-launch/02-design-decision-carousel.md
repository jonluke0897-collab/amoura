# Instagram — Design Decision Carousel

**Use when:** A design decision deserves more visual unpacking than a reel — feature comparisons, before/after copy, schema diagrams.
**Format:** 5–8 slides, square or 4:5 vertical.
**Cadence:** ~1 per 2 weeks during pre-launch.

---

## Slide structure

### Slide 1 — Cover
> **Headline:** "Why [APP NAME] makes you [SPECIFIC ACTION] before you can [OTHER ACTION]"
> **Sub:** "Design decision #[N]"
> **Visual:** Bold type, minimal background. Brand palette only (logo violet/coral).

### Slide 2 — The status quo
> **Headline:** "On most apps:"
> **Body:** "[ONE-SENTENCE DESCRIPTION OF HOW OTHER APPS WORK]"
> **Visual:** Generic interface mock or icon — never a screenshot of a real competitor.

### Slide 3 — What's broken
> **Headline:** "What that does:"
> **Body:** "[ONE-SENTENCE IMPACT — e.g., 'an inbox of one-tap likes from people who didn't read your profile']"
> **Visual:** Icon or simple illustration.

### Slide 4 — What we did
> **Headline:** "In Amia:"
> **Body:** "[ONE-SENTENCE DESCRIPTION OF YOUR DESIGN]"
> **Visual:** Real screen recording / screenshot of the actual feature.

### Slide 5 — Why
> **Headline:** "Because:"
> **Body:** "[ONE-SENTENCE WHY, ATTRIBUTED TO ADVISOR FEEDBACK IF APPLICABLE]"

### Slide 6 (optional) — Quote
> **Visual:** Pull quote from advisor or beta user (with permission).

### Slide 7 — CTA
> **Headline:** "More design decisions every week."
> **Sub:** "Waitlist in bio."

---

## Notes

- Keep the brand palette consistent across slides. Logo violet/coral only — no random accent colors.
- Don't put more than ~20 words per slide. Carousels with text walls don't get swiped through.
- The first slide is the entire engagement test. Make it count.
