# TikTok — Design Decision Explainer (Weeks -7 to -1, recurring)

**Use when:** You shipped a feature or made a design decision that reflects the anti-fetishization thinking. This is the most reliable performing format on TikTok per GTM § 7.
**Length:** 30–60 seconds, face-to-camera with screen-share or product shots cut in.
**Cadence:** ~1 per week through pre-launch.

---

## Script

> **Hook:**
> "Here's why you can't [SPECIFIC GENERIC ACTION OTHER APPS ALLOW — e.g., 'send a generic like'] in the dating app I'm building."

> **Beat 2 — The problem:**
> "On most apps, [DESCRIBE THE STATUS QUO IN ONE SENTENCE — e.g., 'sending a like takes one tap and means nothing']. For trans women, that one tap is what makes the inbox unusable — [SPECIFIC IMPACT — e.g., 'hundreds of low-effort likes from cis men who just clicked because the photo had a trans person in it']."

> **Beat 3 — The fix (show the screen):**
> "So in Amia, [SHOW THE FEATURE]. To send a like, you have to [SPECIFIC REQUIREMENT — e.g., 'pick a specific photo or prompt and write a comment about it']. It takes 30 seconds instead of one. That's the point."

> **Beat 4 — Receipts:**
> "[ADVISOR FIRST NAME] told me [SPECIFIC FEEDBACK FROM AN ADVISOR THAT INFORMED THIS]. So that's what we shipped."

> **Beat 5 — Close:**
> "More design decisions every week. Waitlist in bio."

---

## Caption

```
Design decision #[N]: [ONE-LINE SUMMARY].

Why: [ONE-SENTENCE WHY].

If you want to see what we're building: link in bio.

#trans #t4t #datingapp #productdesign #buildinpublic
```

## Notes

- Show the actual app on screen — not mockups, not Figma. Real screen recording.
- Always credit the advisor by first name (with permission). Pay them for the time it takes to clear the credit.
- If the feature isn't quite shipped yet, label it "shipping this week" — never imply it's live when it isn't.
