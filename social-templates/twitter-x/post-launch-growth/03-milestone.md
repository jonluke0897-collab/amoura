# Twitter/X — Milestone Tweet

**Use when:** A meaningful milestone happens — first 1,000 matches, first city hits density, first profitable month, etc.
**Length:** Single tweet most of the time. Thread only if the milestone has real story behind it.

---

## Single tweet

```
[APP NAME] just hit [SPECIFIC MILESTONE — e.g., "1,000 matches in [CITY]"].

[ONE-LINE CONTEXT — e.g., "Two months ago I wasn't sure we'd hit density in any city. We did."]

Thanks to everyone in [CITY] who's making this happen.

[OPTIONAL LINK]
```

---

## Thread (for bigger milestones)

> **1/**
> Quiet milestone: [APP NAME] just [SPECIFIC THING]. 🧵

> **2/**
> The honest context: [ONE-PARAGRAPH STORY — what made you uncertain about this milestone, what changed, what it actually means].

> **3/**
> The credit: [SPECIFIC PEOPLE / ADVISORS / USERS / VENUES WHO DESERVE TO BE TAGGED]. Without them this is a different number.

> **4/**
> The honest part: [SPECIFIC THING THAT'S STILL NOT WORKING DESPITE THIS NUMBER LOOKING GOOD].

> **5/**
> What's next: [WHAT THIS UNLOCKS, WHAT YOU'RE STILL CHASING].
>
> [LINK]

---

## Notes

- Don't post vanity milestones (followers, likes on a post, app store rank). Only product milestones.
- Always pair the celebration with a "still not working" line — it's truer and it ages better.
- Don't tweet new milestones more than ~1x per month or they stop landing.
