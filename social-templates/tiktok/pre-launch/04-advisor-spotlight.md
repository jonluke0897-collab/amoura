# TikTok — Advisor Spotlight (Weeks -6 onward)

**Use when:** Amplifying an advisor's expertise, work, or a specific contribution. Builds credibility and pays trans expertise its due airtime.
**Length:** 45–75 seconds. Co-produced with the advisor — they should be on camera (with permission and pay) or the script should quote them at length.
**Cadence:** ~1 per month minimum through pre-launch.

---

## Script (co-hosted format)

> **Hook (founder):**
> "This is [ADVISOR FULL NAME OR FIRST NAME]. They're the reason [SPECIFIC FEATURE / DECISION / COPY] in Amia looks the way it does."

> **Beat 2 — Hand-off (advisor on camera):**
> "[ADVISOR DELIVERS THEIR OWN INTRO — e.g., 'I'm a [PROFESSION] in [CITY]. I've been on dating apps for [TIME], and the thing that makes them unusable is [THING].']"

> **Beat 3 — The contribution (advisor):**
> "[ADVISOR DESCRIBES THE SPECIFIC THING THEY PUSHED FOR — in their own words, not yours]."

> **Beat 4 — Founder close:**
> "I'm paying [ADVISOR FIRST NAME] [TRANSPARENT RATE] for this work. If you're trans and you'd be a good advisor — applications open in bio."

---

## Script (founder-only with advisor quoted)

> **Hook:**
> "Today's design comes from a conversation with [ADVISOR FIRST NAME], one of Amia's paid advisors."

> **Beat 2 — Quote them directly:**
> "She told me, quote — '[VERBATIM QUOTE THE ADVISOR HAS APPROVED FOR USE].' End quote."

> **Beat 3 — What changed because of it:**
> "Because of that, [SPECIFIC CHANGE WE MADE]."

> **Beat 4 — Pay receipt:**
> "I paid [ADVISOR FIRST NAME] for the conversation. I'll pay [HER / HIM / THEM] for every conversation."

## Caption

```
Spotlight on [ADVISOR FULL NAME OR FIRST NAME] — paid Amia advisor.

[ONE-SENTENCE CONTRIBUTION].

Pay trans expertise. Always.

[ADVISOR'S OWN HANDLE IF THEY WANT IT TAGGED]

#trans #t4t #datingapp
```

## Notes

- Written, signed consent before filming. Never assume.
- Pay separately for the appearance — this is on top of their retainer, not a credit-against-hours thing.
- If the advisor wants their face blurred or only voice used, do that — no negotiating.
- Let the advisor approve the final cut before posting. This is non-negotiable.
- This counts toward the ≥ 2:1 amplification ratio (GTM § 8).
