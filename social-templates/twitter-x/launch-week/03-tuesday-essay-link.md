# Twitter/X — Tuesday Long-Form Essay Link (Day 2)

**Use when:** Posting the link to the launch-week long-form essay ("Why I built Amia — and how it's designed so fetishization doesn't work") on your own blog.
**Length:** 1 tweet, optionally followed by 2–3 quote-tweets pulling specific paragraphs.

---

## Lead tweet

```
New piece: "[ESSAY TITLE]"

[ONE-LINE TEASE — e.g., "What I'm paying advisors. What I got wrong in week 2. What could still go very wrong."]

[BLOG LINK]
```

---

## Optional follow-up quote tweets (1 per day for 3 days)

Pull specific paragraphs from the essay and quote-tweet the lead tweet with them. Each one should be self-contained:

> Quote 1 — specific advisor numbers:
> "From the essay — [PARAGRAPH ABOUT WHAT YOU PAY ADVISORS, COPY/PASTED]."

> Quote 2 — specific architectural decision:
> "From the essay — [PARAGRAPH EXPLAINING ONE PRODUCT DECISION IN DEPTH]."

> Quote 3 — what could go wrong:
> "From the essay — [PARAGRAPH ABOUT FAILURE MODES YOU'RE WATCHING FOR]."

---

## Notes

- Publish the essay on your own blog (amiadating.com/writing or your personal site), not Medium or Substack as primary — owned property compounds.
- Have an advisor review the essay before publishing.
- Don't tweet the link more than 3x total in the first week.
