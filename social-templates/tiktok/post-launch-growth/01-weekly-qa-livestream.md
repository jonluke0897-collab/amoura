# TikTok — Weekly Q&A Livestream Promo

**Use when:** Promoting your weekly 30-minute founder livestream (see GTM § 5).
**Length:** 20–30 seconds, face-to-camera.
**Cadence:** Weekly, posted ~24 hours before the stream.

---

## Script

> **Hook:**
> "Tomorrow at [TIME] [TIMEZONE] I'm going live for 30 minutes."

> **Beat 2 — What it is:**
> "Open Q&A — anything about Amia, building it, where we are, what's broken, what's next."

> **Beat 3 — One specific thing you'll cover:**
> "I'll walk through [SPECIFIC TOPIC, e.g., 'last week's NPS results — including the one piece of feedback that's making us redesign onboarding']."

> **Beat 4 — Close:**
> "Tomorrow, [TIME] [TIMEZONE], here on TikTok Live. Bring questions."

---

## Caption

```
LIVE tomorrow at [TIME] [TIMEZONE] 🎙

Topic: [ONE-LINE TOPIC].

Q&A about Amia, building it, what we're learning. 30 minutes.

#amia #buildinpublic #trans
```

## Notes

- Pick one specific topic per stream — generic "ask me anything" gets fewer questions than "here's what we're working on, what do you want to know about it?"
- Save key answers as 30-second clips for the next 2–3 days of content.
