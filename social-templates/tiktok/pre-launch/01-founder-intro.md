# TikTok — Founder Intro (Week -8)

**Use when:** Your very first post on the @amiadating or founder TikTok handle. Sets the posture for everything that follows.
**Length:** 60–75 seconds, face-to-camera, no music, no cuts mid-sentence.

---

## Script

> **Hook (first 3 seconds — must stop the scroll):**
> "I built a dating app for trans women and the people who want to date them. Here's why."

> **Beat 2 — Why (10–15s):**
> "Here's why I'm doing it: [SPECIFIC PERSONAL REASON OR OBSERVATION — e.g., 'every trans woman I know has been harassed off Tinder, Hinge, and Bumble']. The apps that exist for trans women aren't actually built for them — they're built around them."

> **Beat 3 — The honest part (15–20s):**
> "I'm not the expert on this experience. I never will be. So here's what I'm doing about that: I'm hiring [NUMBER] paid trans advisors at [HOURLY RATE] an hour. They review every piece of copy, every design decision, every moderation rule. If they say something's wrong, it changes."

> **Beat 4 — The ask (10s):**
> "If you're trans and you've ever wanted a dating app that takes a side — I'd love your feedback. Link in bio for the waitlist. And if you want to be a paid advisor, my DMs are open."

> **Beat 5 — The promise (5s):**
> "I'll keep posting as I build. The good and the embarrassing."

## Notes

- Do NOT add background music. Voice + face only — anything else reads as marketed.
- Do NOT use the word "ally."
- Pin this video to your profile until launch week.
- Have an advisor review the script before filming.
