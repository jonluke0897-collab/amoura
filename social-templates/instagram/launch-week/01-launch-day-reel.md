# Instagram — Launch Day Reel (Monday)

**Use when:** Launch day. Recut of the TikTok launch-day video, adapted for IG.
**Post time:** 8am PT (1 hour after TikTok/Twitter, to space the cross-channel push).
**Length:** 45–60 seconds.

---

## Script

(Same beats as `tiktok/launch-week/02-monday-launch-day.md`.)

> **Hook:**
> "Today I'm launching [APP NAME] — a dating app for trans women and the people who want to date them."

> **Beat 2 — Differentiator 1:**
> "You can't send a generic like. Every like has to include a comment on a specific photo or prompt."

> **Beat 3 — Differentiator 2:**
> "Trans-for-trans is a first-class control, not a hidden filter."

> **Beat 4 — Differentiator 3:**
> "Cis users sign an extended respect pledge before they can match."

> **Beat 5 — Close:**
> "Available in [LAUNCH CITY 1], [LAUNCH CITY 2], [LAUNCH CITY 3] starting today. iOS + Android. Link in bio."
